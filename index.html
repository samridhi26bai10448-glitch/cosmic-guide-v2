Learn to Find Constellations

Never looked at a constellation before? Start here!

1. Find a dark spot

Choose a safe outdoor place away from bright lights. Give your eyes 15–20 minutes to adjust to the darkness.

2. Look for a familiar pattern

Start with the Big Dipper, a group of seven bright stars shaped a little like a ladle. It is part of Ursa Major, but its visibility depends on your location.

3. Connect the stars

Imagine lines between the brighter stars to recognise a pattern. These lines are guides; they are not actually drawn in the sky.

4. Match the sky

Use a star map set to your location and current time. Compare its star patterns with the real sky.

5. Be patient

Clouds, moonlight and city lights can hide faint stars. Start with one pattern at a time, and never look directly at the Sun.
